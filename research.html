Aging & Cardiovascular Disease

How biological aging reshapes vascular and cardiac health.

We investigate how aging alters inflammatory signaling, endothelial function, vascular integrity, cellular state, and cardiac performance. Our work integrates human data, computational approaches, and iPSC-based cardiovascular models to define mechanisms of cardiovascular aging.

We are particularly interested in how aging intersects with genetic susceptibility, environmental stressors, and chronic inflammation to influence cardiovascular resilience and disease progression.
