Research Program

Vascular Biology & Regeneration

Decoding the endothelium in health, disease, and regeneration.

Overview

Our scientific focus

The vascular endothelium is an active signaling interface that senses mechanical, inflammatory, metabolic, and environmental cues. We study how endothelial dysfunction and plasticity contribute to cardiovascular disease and how vascular function can be restored.

Endothelial dysfunctionIntegrated human models and mechanistic studies.
Endothelial plasticity & EndoMTIntegrated human models and mechanistic studies.
Vascular regenerationIntegrated human models and mechanistic studies.
Central Question

Which endothelial states drive disease, and which pathways can be targeted to restore vascular homeostasis and regenerative capacity?

Our Approach

From human disease to mechanism.

Our studies integrate patient-derived vascular specimens, iPSC-derived endothelial cells, functional vascular phenotyping, genome engineering, and multi-omic profiling to identify regulatory programs controlling vascular injury and repair.
