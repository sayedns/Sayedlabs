Join Us

Build the next generation of human cardiovascular models.

We welcome scientists interested in cardiovascular biology, human stem cells, organoids, genome engineering, single-cell and spatial genomics, and translational medicine.

Postdoctoral Fellows

We welcome inquiries from prospective fellows interested in cardiomyopathy, vascular biology, cardio-oncology, human iPSC disease modeling, organoids, functional genomics, single-cell/spatial multi-omics and environmental cardiovascular disease.

Please send: CV, brief description of prior research, research interests, career goals and references.

Graduate Students

Stanford graduate students interested in cardiovascular biology, stem cell biology, genomics, bioengineering and translational research are encouraged to contact the lab about rotations and projects.

Undergraduate Researchers

We support motivated Stanford undergraduates seeking sustained research experiences in biomedical science.

Mentorship

Our goal is to help trainees ask important questions, design rigorous experiments, communicate clearly, compete for independent funding and develop their own scientific identity.
